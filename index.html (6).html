Text or call to mark attendance. Your number is your ID.

Link your mobile number to your user ID once. In this interactive demo, simulated texts and calls use the number you enter to find its linked user ID. Live SMS/call attendance requires a backend and a messaging/telephony provider.

Prototype note: this phone simulator is not connected to a carrier. Real incoming SMS/calls require a backend webhook and a messaging/telephony provider; verify the provider-supplied sender number before recording attendance.
